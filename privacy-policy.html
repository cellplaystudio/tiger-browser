Privacy Policy for Tiger Browser

Effective date: 27 November 2026

App: Tiger Browser (com.cellplaystudio.tigerbrowser)

Published by: Cell Play Studio ("we", "us", "our")

Contact: cellplaystudio@gmail.com

This policy explains what Tiger Browser does with information, in plain language. The short version: Tiger has no accounts, collects no analytics and no crash reports, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your personal data. Your browsing data stays on your phone. A few features need to contact other companies' servers to work; they are listed in section 4 so you can decide for yourself.

1. What we collect

Nothing about you. We do not run servers that receive information from the app. Specifically, Tiger does not:

  • require an account, a sign-in or an email address;
  • collect analytics, usage statistics, advertising IDs or device identifiers;
  • send crash reports or diagnostic data to us;
  • show advertising or share data with advertisers;
  • track you across apps or websites.

Because we receive no personal data, we cannot look up, export or delete data about you on our side. There is none.

2. What is stored on your phone

Like every browser, Tiger keeps data on your device so it can work: your open tabs, browsing history, bookmarks, cookies and site data, saved passwords and autofill entries (if you use them), downloads, settings and the dashboard background you choose. This data stays on your device, protected by Android. You can remove it at any time in Settings (clear browsing data) or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Files you save with the media saver are stored in your phone's Downloads/Tiger folder. They are yours; delete them like any other file.

3. Permissions

Tiger asks only for what a feature needs:

  • Internet and network state: to load pages. Change network state: to let you switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data when a page will not load; this only happens when you tap the button.
  • Notifications: to show download progress and, if you allow it, website notifications.
  • Location, camera and microphone: only when a website you are visiting asks and you allow it; you can change this in Site settings. We never read them ourselves.
  • Storage / media: to save the files you choose to download.

4. Information that goes to other companies when you use a feature

Tiger is built on an open-source browser engine. Some features work by contacting third-party servers. They receive the usual technical information that any request carries (your IP address, the time, the app version and the operating system) and the data described below. We do not receive a copy.

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Websites you visitYour requests go to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. Tiger blocks many ads, trackers and known malware sites by default.
SearchWhat you type in the address bar or search box is sent to your chosen search engine (Google by default) to give you results and suggestions. Google's privacy policy applies. You can change the search engine in Settings.
Secure DNSWhen on (the default), the names of the sites you visit are looked up through an encrypted DNS service (Cloudflare or Google by default) instead of your network's. Their privacy policies apply.
Ad blocking, component updates, site iconsTiger downloads its ad-block and malware lists and its browser components from Tiger's own update service (run on Cloudflare). It receives the update check (app and component versions) and, like any server, your IP address; it keeps no logs. For browser components made by Google (such as the certificate revocation list) and for site icons, it asks Google's public services on your behalf, without your IP address, cookies or other headers. Tiger has no safe-browsing warnings from Google and no page translation.
Spell-checkUses your phone's own spell checker; Tiger downloads no dictionaries.
Media saverWhen you open the save picker, Tiger asks the website that hosts a file for its type and size (a small request with no body) and downloads the files you choose from that site.

Tiger is designed so that analytics, crash reporting and usage pings of the underlying engine are switched off.

5. Advertising and paid features

Tiger currently shows no advertising and has no paid features. If a future version adds ads or a paid "Pro" plan, we will update this policy before it takes effect, describe exactly what data is involved, and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Purchases, if any, are handled by Google Play; we do not see your payment details.

6. Children

Tiger is a general-audience browser and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect information from children, and as explained above we collect no personal information from anyone.

7. Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by Android's security. Connections to websites use HTTPS where the site supports it, and Secure DNS is on by default. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and your software up to date.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA and UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to not have your data sold or shared. We do not sell or share personal data and we hold none about you, so there is nothing for us to return or erase; you control the data on your device directly in Settings. For data held by the third parties in section 4, please contact them under their own policies. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. You can write to us any time at cellplaystudio@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Because we do not receive your data, we do not transfer it. The third parties in section 4 operate internationally and may process requests in other countries under their own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version; for material changes we will also tell you in the app. Continuing to use Tiger after a change means you accept the updated policy.
